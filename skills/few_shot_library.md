Should I rent a skid steer or hire a contractor to clear my property?


Rent if you're clearing under an acre with light vegetation. Stuff like palmetto, brush, saplings under 2-3 inches in diameter. Hire out if you've got dense timber, large trees, or more than an acre to tackle.


The equipment changes completely once you move beyond light clearing. A John Deere 317G handles brush perfectly but struggles with anything over 3 inches or root systems from mature trees.


Factor in disposal too. Contractors handle debris removal and burning permits. DIY projects often underestimate cleanup time and costs.


Got a job coming up and not sure what you need? Call or message us. We're happy to help you figure it out before you commit to anything.
